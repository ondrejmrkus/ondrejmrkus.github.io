About

This website is my digital garage where I share my work.
2 min · Ondrej Markus

Hello again. I’m Ondrej, a designer who just moved to Amsterdam.

I’m an introvert, generalist, self-learner and gamer.

This website is my digital garage where I share my articles and reflections about design, games and self-improvement.

Core beliefs#

1/ Spend your life doing something you enjoy.#

Seeing people go about their lives doing work they don’t like makes me sad and angry. Everyone has the potential to make a living doing work they love. It might not be easy, but it’s more possible than you think. And it’s very much worth the effort.

2/ Try your best in everything you do.#

Do your best but don’t be afraid of making mistakes. Mistakes are necessary for learning. Don’t wait for perfection. There is no such thing. Find the courage to be imperfect and take the first step.

3/ Don’t take yourself too seriously.#

Life and work should be enjoyable. Don’t lose a good night’s sleep over things you cannot change. Play the game of life well and have fun.
