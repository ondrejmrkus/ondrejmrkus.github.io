About

February 6, 2022 · 3 min · Ondrej Markus

This website is a sincere effort of one introvert on a mission to help humans create simple systems that make us happy and productive.

What’s the deal here#

My name is Ondrej. I’m a 27-year-old entrepreneur, designer, and indie writer living in Prague. Also generalist, introvert, and self-learner. Big fan of systems and games.

And this is my digital garage with the doors kept open.

I love deconstructing complex ideas into easy-to-use methods and tools.

That’s why I write and share two articles every week that explore new ways to help myself and others live a better life.

Among the topics are:

  • Life design: How to systematically create a better life for yourself?
  • Self-management: How to manage your time and make better decisions?
  • Learning better: How to learn new things more effectively?
  • Entrepreneurship: How to make a living building things people want?
  • Writing & creativity: How to write and create consistently to cultivate your creative life?

I run this site and create things with a couple of ideas in mind that express the spirit of my work better than anything else:

1/ Find meaningful work you enjoy doing.#

Seeing people waste their lives doing work they don’t like makes me sad and angry. Everyone has the potential to make a living doing work they love. It’s not easy, but it’s more possible than you think. And it’s very much worth the effort.

2/ Try your best in everything you do.#

But don’t be afraid of making mistakes. Mistakes are necessary for learning. Do your best but don’t wait for perfection. There is no such thing. Find the courage to be imperfect.

3/ Don’t take yourself too seriously.#

Life and work should be enjoyable. All of this is a game worth playing well, but don’t lose a good night’s sleep over things you cannot change. Play the game well and have fun.
