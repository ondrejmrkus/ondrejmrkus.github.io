Right now, everything on this site is a labor of pure love and a burning curiosity for the topics I write about, and how they can help us live better lives.

### Self-funded

I fund this site and my writing time from my own pockets. I'm not going to starve for at least 5 more months because I teach an entrepreneurship course at a local university, which _partially_ covers my monthly living costs.

Although, I'd like to move above minimum wage soon_-ish_ while continuing to spend most of my time writing. And I'm doing my best to make it happen.

It's crazy to imagine I could be doing this full-time because people like you decided to make it happen. Thank you for even considering it.
