## My work now

I like to move fast, try many things, and learn by doing. No job ever satisfied my learning appetite for more than 3-6 months. I need the freedom to choose my own projects.

2 years ago, I fell in love with writing. I enjoy the craft so much.

Also, I love the lifestyle that comes with it: Working independently. Complete control over my schedule. No meetings. Free to wrestle with weird ideas all day long, every day.

I feel guilty saying this because it doesn't feel like having a real job. It feels like play. It's playing like a pro, sure, I work even when I don't feel like it. But still, it's so much fun.

Right now, I'm working full-time to make a living as an independent writer. I don't know the shape or form it's going to take, and I don't care as long as I'm able to keep writing.
