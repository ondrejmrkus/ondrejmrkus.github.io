Brewing morning coffee is my favorite ritual of the day.

Once I tasted the good stuff, I was hooked. There is no way back to those 3-in-1 instant coffee sticks I sipped while playing League of Legends in highschool.

### My current setup

I grind beans with a manual grinder and brew them with a Hario V60 coffee set I like to look at.
