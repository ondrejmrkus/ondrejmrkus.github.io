## Core beliefs

### 1/ Spend your life doing something you enjoy.

Seeing people doing work they don't like makes me sad and angry. Everyone can make a living doing work they enjoy. It might not be easy, but it's more possible than we think. Go for it.

### 2/ Try your best in everything you do.

Do your best but don't be afraid of making mistakes. Mistakes are a necessary part of learning. Don't wait for perfection. There is no such thing. Find the courage to be imperfect and try your best.

### 3/ Don't take yourself too seriously.

Life and work should be pleasant. Things might get hard, but don't lose a good night's sleep over things you cannot change. Life is a game. Do your best but don't forget to have fun.
