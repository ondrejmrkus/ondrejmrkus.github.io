I'm looking for a place to start. That's always difficult because I feel like the first step, the first project, is crucial.

In reality, it isn't. The really important thing is to start moving. Make anything. Don't think too much about whether it's good because it probably isn't, and that's okay.

So what's the project for today?

Inspiration struck me on last weekend's board game retreat with my friends. We played [Brass Birmingham](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/224517/brass-birmingham) for most of the weekend and it was great fun. But that's not where the inspiration came from.

There is an old copy of [Stone Age](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/34635/stone-age) in the cabbot of the house we stayed at several times in the past. And somebody always wants to play it. I kinda want to play it. Even though it's a pretty simple game you can "solve" after playing it a couple of times, which we did.

![Stone Age](/images/2022/07/stone-age.png)

However. There is something deeply satisfying about rolling a bunch of dice in a cup over and over again. It's fun to send less workers for food than math and safety dictates to make sure you have enough to feed your clan. You shake the dice, yell something like "C'mon, daddy needs some sixes baby!" and you smash the cup onto the table. After a second of hesitation, you reveal the dice, and the second before you know whether it worked or not is thrilling. What follows is a big cheer of joy or curses on your greed and the fact that members of your clan will eat your precious stone for dinner.

This is where the game of Stone Age resides. This makes it satisfying to play, even if you've solved the rest. You can just roll dice again and again, and enjoy the simple pleasure of suspense before revealing the dice and how it shifts into cheers or screams.

It's the thrill of gambling but without loosing your house in the process.

So, the idea is to build a dice game around this mechanic: rolling a bunch of dice with the same moment of suspense and payoff. That's the core.

As a start, I'm just gonna steal a couple more ideas from the original and see where the prototyping process takes me.

On paper, it's a simple worker placement dice game where you put your workers on cards with resources (like in Stone Age) and then roll the number of dice equal to how many workers you sent there.

![My prototype no. 1](/images/2022/07/dd-prototype-1.jpeg)

I wonder if I can find a way to simplify the rest of the original game. It has a big board, options beside the resource collections, and pretty elaborate scoring system.

I would like to take the core loop of collecting resources through putting workers on little spots and rolling dice. And I want to eliminate as much of the rest as possible, while adding only things that make the game better.

We'll see. This is a really improvised post in the morning of the first day I started working on this, so it's very raw. But that's the idea of these design diares.

Let's practice.
